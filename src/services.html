common questions

How big a system do I need?

It depends on what you want to run and for how long. Count your appliances in the size guide on our contact page for a rough idea; we confirm it after a site visit.

Can solar carry my air conditioner?

Yes, if the system is sized for it. Customers with our 7.5 kVA systems run their AC and fridge on solar. Tell us the size of your AC and we will advise.

Lithium or tubular batteries?

Tubular costs less up front. Lithium costs more but lasts much longer, takes less space and needs almost no maintenance. We will show you both and explain the difference in naira over the years.

Can you fix a system another installer put in?

Yes. A lot of our work is correcting and upgrading other people's installations. We check the wiring, the sizing and the batteries, then tell you what needs to change.

Do I need to come to the shop?

No. Call or WhatsApp and we come to you. You are welcome to visit 79 Akpakpava Road, opposite Union Bank, to see the equipment first.
