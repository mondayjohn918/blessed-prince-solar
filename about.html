A home of light, on Akpakpava Road.

Blessed Prince B. Solar System, also known as Prince Bishop Enterprises, sells and installs solar systems and inverters from our shop opposite Union Bank, Benin City.

Light every hour, not just when NEPA allows.

Blessed Prince B. was started by Prince Chibueze Bishop Odenigbo with one simple promise painted on the signboard: 24/7 light. In a city where the grid comes and goes, that promise means a fridge that stays cold, a shop that stays open and children who can read at night.

We are dealers in solar batteries, panels, inverters, street lights, thunder arresters, charge controllers and solar boreholes. Because we sell at company prices and install ourselves, you deal with one team from the first visit to the last service call.

Most of our customers come through other customers. That is why we take neatness seriously, size systems honestly and pick up the phone when someone calls.
