What 24/7 light looks like, hour by hour.

06:00

The sun comes up. So do the panels.

As soon as there is daylight, the panels start feeding the house and topping up the batteries. No generator, no fuel queue.

12:00

Midday runs on the roof.

Fridge, fans, TV and even the AC run on sunshine, while the extra power fills the batteries for tonight.

18:00

NEPA takes light. You barely notice.

The inverter switches over by itself. The lights stay on, the fridge keeps humming, and nobody goes looking for the generator key.

00:00

The batteries carry you to morning.

A well-sized battery bank keeps fans, lights and the fridge going all night. Then the sun comes up and it starts again.
